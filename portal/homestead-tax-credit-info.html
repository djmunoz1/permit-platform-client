Homestead Tax Credit Eligibility Application (HST)

For Homeowners

Maryland requires all homeowners to submit a one-time application to establish eligibility for the Homestead Tax Credit.

The credit is applied automatically to your property taxes each year after you're approved.

Learn more about the Homestead Tax Credit

Before you apply

  • You must own and live in your home

    The credit is for your principal residence only.

  • It's a one-time application

    Once approved, you'll receive the credit each year automatically.

  • Have your information ready

    You may need your property address, Social Security number, and income details.
